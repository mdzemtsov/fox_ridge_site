# Track Record Figure Reconciliation

**Status:** Internal working record for public-content controls.

**Scope:** Our Team, Track Record, and the reusable principal-experience proof blocks in public React pages.
**Decision date:** August 21, 2026.

## Public Content Decision

FoxRidge will use the **selected high-level historical proof** model on the public Track Record page. The page will state that the proof reflects the principals’ prior activities under prior sponsoring entities where applicable. It will not publish deal-level IRR, equity-multiple, purchase-price, sale-price, or performance-return data in the client-side page source, and it will not present a browser-only checkbox as a formal access-control mechanism.

A confidential introduction is the appropriate public request path for any discussion of additional information. Whether further information is available, and on what terms, remains subject to internal review, applicable requirements, and definitive documentation.

## Unresolved Figure and Required Withholding

The previous Our Team page stated that Slava Davidenko had “successfully exited **8 full-cycle deals** as a General Partner.” The active Track Record source supplies data for **six** selected realized projects and does not supply source records that reconcile the additional two exits. The public-facing reference to eight exits must therefore be removed until FoxRidge provides verified supporting information that establishes the scope, dates, entities, and relationship to the six selected projects.

The figure **1,347 units currently under management** and the individual active-holdings descriptions were also tied to the former detailed Track Record presentation. They are not required for the selected high-level historical-proof model and should not remain on the revised public Track Record page without a separately verified current-platform basis.

## Principal Experience Methodology

The public principal-experience block may retain the owner-approved figures of **$1B+ transaction experience**, **7,000+ units**, and **36 properties** only with one methodology note:

> “Combined transaction experience, units, and properties reflect the principals’ prior activities under prior sponsoring entities where applicable. These figures are historical experience metrics, not a representation of FoxRidge’s own track record.”

The public pages must also state:

> “Past performance is not indicative of future results.”

## Cross-Site Controls

| Location | Public treatment |
|---|---|
| Our Team | Evidence-led role descriptions; common principal-experience methodology note; no unverified eight-exit reference. |
| Track Record | Public selected high-level historical proof only; no client-side gate and no detailed performance table in source. |
| Home, Capital Partners, International Investors | Keep the approved $1B+, 7,000+, and 36-property experience context only where accompanied by the common prior-entity methodology and past-performance wording. |
| Investor Portal and controlled presentations | Not reconciled by this document. Existing static portal materials require separate owner-and-counsel review because browser-only access screens do not secure direct static asset URLs. |

## Evidence Gap Requiring Owner Input

To restore any statement of **eight full-cycle exits**, detailed deal-level return data, or a current active-portfolio total, FoxRidge needs a verified internal source that identifies the relevant transactions, principal role, prior sponsoring entity, units, timing, and the intended disclosure scope. Until then, these items remain withheld from the public proof page.
