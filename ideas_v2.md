# Selected Approach: "Swiss Tech / Architectural"

I will proceed with the **"Swiss Tech / Architectural"** approach. This strikes the perfect balance between "Modern/Interesting" and the professional credibility required for an investment firm. It avoids the "corporate blue" cliché while remaining high-end. The "Deep Forest" and "Copper" palette is distinctive and rich.

**Design Philosophy**: A digital structure that feels like a modern architectural masterpiece—clean lines, premium materials, and thoughtful engineering.

**Style Guide for Development**:
- **Colors**: 
  - Primary: Deep Forest Green (`#052e16` - green-950) or Dark Charcoal (`#1c1917` - stone-900)
  - Secondary: Copper/Clay (`#c2410c` - orange-700) or Burnt Sienna
  - Background: Mist/Off-white (`#f5f5f4` - stone-100) or pure white
  - Text: Dark Stone (`#1c1917` - stone-900)
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: `Space Grotesk` (Google Font) - Distinctive, modern, slightly tech.
  - Body: `DM Sans` (Google Font) - Highly readable, geometric.
- **Components**:
  - "Bento box" grid layouts
  - 1px borders
  - Large typography
  - Subtle noise textures
